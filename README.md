# Prevalence, Determinants, and Healthcare Utilization of Multimorbidity among Farmers in Tamil Nadu

## Project Overview
A community-based cross-sectional study investigating the prevalence, determinants, and healthcare utilization patterns associated with multimorbidity among farmers in selected areas of Tiruchirappalli district, Tamil Nadu.

## Study Design
- Study type: Community-based cross-sectional study
- Study population: Farmers aged 18 years and above
- Sample size: 300 participants
- Study location: Selected areas of Tiruchirappalli district, Tamil Nadu, India

## Research Objectives
1. Estimate the prevalence of multimorbidity among farmers.
2. Assess sociodemographic, lifestyle, and occupational determinants of multimorbidity.
3. Compare healthcare utilization and out-of-pocket healthcare expenditure by multimorbidity status.
